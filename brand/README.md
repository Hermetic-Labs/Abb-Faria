# Abbé Faria visual system

The original concept is preserved at `brand/reference/abbe-faria-concept.png`.
It is a directional reference rather than a production logo.

## Extracted visual grammar

- A human figure is centered in front of an institutional gateway.
- Open arms and broken chains express capability restored without pretending
  that the surrounding institution has disappeared.
- A circular orbit represents the relationship between human, system, and
  institution.
- Red, green, and blue divide the orbit into three controlled signal paths.
- Warm ivory carries the human form; near-black carries the infrastructure.
- Four-point stars mark attention, intelligence, and an opening rather than a
  magical effect.

## Current production mark

`dist/assets/abbe-faria-mark-512.png` is a transparent, simplified interpretation
for the website. The name remains live HTML text rather than baked into the
image, so spelling, accessibility, and responsive sizing stay deterministic.

## Regeneration prompt

Built-in image generation prompt used for the current mark:

> Create a simplified, reproducible emblem derived from the reference image's
> core visual grammar: one abstract androgynous human glyph standing before a
> rectangular gateway, arms opening outward, with one short broken chain from
> each wrist; a thin circular orbit divided into red at the top, green at lower
> left, and blue at lower right; a small four-point star above the head and at
> the chest. Use a minimal flat vector-like style with a strong silhouette,
> crisp geometry, balanced negative space, warm off-white and near-black forms,
> and a genuinely transparent background. No text, gradients, glow, 3D, sparks,
> mockup, or watermark. Keep the mark legible at small size.

The final identity should eventually be redrawn as vector paths from this
construction rather than traced from generated pixels.

## Supporting image prompts

- `dist/assets/secure-edge-suite.webp`: editorial product scene of a rugged
  learning tablet, compact fanless edge computer, secure network appliance, and
  modular power cartridge in a controlled institutional technology room. No
  people, bars, handcuffs, logos, readable text, or science-fiction interfaces.
- `dist/assets/controlled-api-path.webp`: physical architectural visualization
  of an approved signal crossing a controlled gateway into a local intelligence
  core, then branching to four managed endpoints. No text, logos, literal locks,
